The following are used as ideas only, and no text is taken from them: published decision-record formats (the decision, its context, the options set aside, who decided), the DACI and RAPID role models (the person who decides is kept apart from the people who give input), and a published study of how meeting assistants go wrong (confident rewording, unsupported numbers, the wrong speaker, tentative talk reported as decided).
