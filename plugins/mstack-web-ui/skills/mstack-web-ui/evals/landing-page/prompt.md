Build a landing page for Example Pantry, a shared grocery list app for people who live together. Write it as one HTML file, `index.html`, with its CSS.

- Who it is for: flatmates and couples in their twenties and thirties who share a kitchen.
- Tone: warm, plain, a little dry. Not corporate.
- Three facts you may use: the list updates for everyone as soon as one person ticks an item; it works without a signal and catches up later; it is free for a household of up to four people.

The page needs a short top bar with the name and a menu button that shows only an icon, a first screen that says what Example Pantry is, the product picture `product.svg` from this folder, and a waiting-list form with an e-mail field and a button. The form is not connected to anything yet. The page has no animation.
