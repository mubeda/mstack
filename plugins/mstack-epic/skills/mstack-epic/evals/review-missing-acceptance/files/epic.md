# Epic: Invoices

Status: Draft
Review: same-vendor, round 1, Ready
Source: docs/product/2026-11-01-self-service-portal/product-plan.md (approved), epic 2

## Differences from the product plan
- Export of the invoice list moved out of this epic.

## Outcome
A finance contact finds and downloads any invoice without writing to support.

## Who it is for
Finance contacts at customer companies.

## In and out
In: list, search and download of invoices. Out: paying an invoice, and export of the list.

## Acceptance
None yet.

## Measurable result
60% of invoice requests to support stop within three months of launch.

## Size
Large, it removes most of the support work.

## Fixed limits
- Live before the price change on 2027-03-01.

## Depends on
Epic 1, sign in for finance contacts.

## User stories
- As a finance contact, I want to download last month's invoice, so that I can book it without waiting for support.
- As a finance contact, I want to search by invoice number, so that I can answer my auditor.

## Open questions for developers
- Can the billing system produce invoice files older than two years?
