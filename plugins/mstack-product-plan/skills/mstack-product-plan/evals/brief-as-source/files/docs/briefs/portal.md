# Design brief: Customer self-service portal

Status: Approved 2026-10-30 (approved in chat by the user)

## Intent
Customers of the billing product should get invoices and change their details without writing to support.

## Goals, non-goals and anti-goals
Goal: fewer support tickets about invoices. Non-goal: online payment.

## Constraints
Live before 2027-03-01.

## Approaches considered
One portal for finance contacts. A chat assistant was rejected.

## Tradeoffs accepted
None.

## Decisions
### Decided by the user
- The portal is for finance contacts at customer companies only.
- Online payment is out.
- It has to be live before 2027-03-01.
### Agent's calls
- Sign in uses the work email.

None.
