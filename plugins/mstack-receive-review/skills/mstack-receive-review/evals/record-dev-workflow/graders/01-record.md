The reply gives the review the record `cross-vendor` in two places: in the log entry of the finding (as `record: cross-vendor`, or under a column or label named Record) and in the tally per source. A reply that shows the record in only one of the two fails. No line of the reply records this review as same-vendor, as a fallback review, as `writer unknown` or as `reviewer unknown`.
