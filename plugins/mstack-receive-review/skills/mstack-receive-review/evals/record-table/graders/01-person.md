The reply gives review 1 the record `-` (a person reviewed) in two places: in the log entry of its finding (a finding merged from several reviews passes when its entry names review 1 with this record) and in the tally per source. A reply that shows the record in only one of the two fails, and so does one that gives review 1 any other record.
